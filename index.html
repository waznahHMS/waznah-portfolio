01 ABOUT ME

Who I Am

BACKGROUND

I'm Waznah binti Haji Mohd Sallehin — an interdisciplinary professional who bridges the gap between technology, policy, and human-centered design. My journey began with a diploma in Network Engineering and has evolved into a broader exploration of how digital tools can serve communities meaningfully.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in History and International Studies at Universiti Brunei Darussalam, I bring a unique perspective to problem-solving — one that values context, people, and stories. I believe great design is not just about how it looks, but how it makes people feel.

I'm the founder of i.lec Enterprise, an AI-powered preventive mental wellness platform, and co-founder of MindBridge, a practitioner-client management system for mental health. I've also been selected for the AI for Asia Fellowship (2026 cohort) and the KESAN Catalyst Plus youth leadership program.
